# Phase 1: SSE west wing

The demo is a deliberately limited 31-vertex subset, not a complete inventory of the two floors. It has 34 edges: 15 on Plaza, 16 on First, and 3 between floors.

## Sources and scale

- Plaza: `University maps/SCHOOL OF SCIENCES AND ENGINEERING/20220808 - SSE USER PLANS - BASE DRAWING - PLAZA FLOOR-Layout1.pdf`.
- First: `University maps/SCHOOL OF SCIENCES AND ENGINEERING/20220808 - SSE USER PLANS - BASE DRAWING - FIRST FLOOR-Layout1.pdf`.

Both sheets are 1:200, as indicated in their title blocks. Positions are approximate points on the displayed, rotated page (3370 x 2384 points), chosen near doors, landings and corridor centers. Indoor edge weights are estimates measured from the plan along the selected corridor polylines, rounded to integer points. One unit is approximately 0.0706 m. The sketch preserves the displayed page orientation and uses these same coordinates.

The inter-floor stair weight 113 (approximately 8 m) and elevator weight 212 (approximately 15 m including wait) are requested estimates, not measurements. ET03 is marked as a freight elevator; its accessibility is **to verify**. The demo treats it as accessible as directed.
